description: Prepares one job end to end - score, truthful tailored CV, notes and one-click apply kit. Use for "tailor my CV for <link>", "prepare an application for this job", or a pasted job description.

Use the `job-finder` skill on the job the person gave (link or pasted description). First check setup is complete
(section 0); if not, run setup instead. Then gate and score it, and do sections 2 and 3. Finish with the score,
gaps, red flags and anything they need to confirm.
